Privacy Policy

Smart Selector browser extension · Last updated: November 23, 2026

Smart Selector ("the extension") is operated by RamziAch19 ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the extension collects, how it is used, and how you can ask us to delete it.

1. What the extension does

The extension reads the numbers in a table on the web page you are viewing and selects the rows that add up to a target amount you enter. An account is required, and access is activated by the owner after payment.

2. Data we collect and store

DataWhy we need it
Account email and your password (the password is handled and hashed by our authentication provider; we cannot read it)To create and secure your account and sign you in
Subscription details: plan, status, expiry date, plan requests, last-seen time, and any note the owner addsTo decide whether your account is active and which features your plan includes
Usage records: for each selection you run, the time, the number of rows in the table, the number of rows selected, the total of the selected amounts, your target amount, the method used and your row preferenceTo show your activity in your account page, and to help the owner understand usage and support you
Payment records entered manually by the owner: date, amount, method and planTo keep a record of your subscription payments

We do not collect your browsing history, and we do not read any page other than the table you ask the extension to analyse.

3. What stays on your device

The contents of the page and its table (names, references, individual row values) are processed locally in your browser and are never sent to us. Only the summary numbers listed under "Usage records" are sent. Your sign-in session, theme choice and preferences are kept in the extension's local browser storage.

4. Where data is stored and who processes it

Account, subscription, usage and payment data are stored with Supabase (hosted database and authentication provider), which acts as our data processor. If you choose to contact the owner through a messaging app (for example WhatsApp or Telegram) to activate your account, that conversation is governed by that service's own policy.

5. How we use data

We use your data only to provide, secure and support the extension. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes, and we do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending decisions. Data is shared only where required by law.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. To delete your account and the associated subscription and usage records, email us at the address below from your registered email. We will delete them within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days. Payment records may be retained where we are required to keep accounting records by law.

7. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. Your account page in the extension shows your plan, expiry, usage and payments.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS, and access to the database is restricted by security rules so that each user can read only their own records. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

9. Children

The extension is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.
